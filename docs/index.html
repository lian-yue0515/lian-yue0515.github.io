About

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Shandong University. My research focuses on robust localization and mapping for mobile robots, with an emphasis on LiDAR-inertial SLAM, degeneracy-aware state estimation, multi-map fusion, and long-term localization.

I work across research and deployment. I have developed open-source SLAM systems including MM-LINS, NVMS-SLAM, ACE-LIO and D-LI-Init, and have experience transferring research prototypes to industrial AGVs. I am also exploring vision-language-action models and embodied robot navigation.
